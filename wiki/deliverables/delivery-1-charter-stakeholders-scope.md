# Delivery 1 — Project Charter, Stakeholder Analysis, WBS

First version of the SmartXCharge Project Plan, written in [[pm-template-d1]] ([[delivery-1-instructions]]). Mandatory, receives feedback, **not directly graded** — but its assumptions carry into D2 and D3.

## Submission

- Single PDF in Fénix, `GPE26-XX-D1.pdf` (XX = group number).
- **Due 18 Nov 2026, 23:59** per the instructions; course slides say 20 Nov ([[epm-course]] — meet the earlier date).

## Checklist

- [ ] Identification table: group number, student names and IDs (those who did the work).
- [ ] **Project Charter** — Sponsor and PM fields = **AES** roles (Supplier Executive Sponsor, Supplier PM); customer = LightEnergy; start/finish dates from the Week 0 = 4 Jan 2027 frame; goal; business benefits; PM success criteria with metrics; success factors with responsible party; intermediate goals; products/deliverables; requirements/specifications; assumptions and constraints; **Initial Internal Cost Estimate** (AES cost, not €650k) by category. See [[project-charter]].
- [ ] **Stakeholder Analysis** — seven-column table; include client governance (COO sponsor, Client PM, Product Owner Energy & Fleet, Client IT/Security, Steering Committee) plus AES roles and other affected parties. See [[stakeholder-analysis]].
- [ ] **WBS** — Annex A5 phases kept as top level; add all AES work packages, including project-management packages. See [[work-breakdown-structure]].
- [ ] **WBS Dictionary** — description + completion criterion/milestone for every work package.
- [ ] Assumptions stated explicitly and recorded for reuse in D2/D3.

## Pitfalls flagged by the sources

- Treating the €650k as the cost estimate ([[fixed-price-contract-and-internal-cost-baseline]]).
- Putting client roles in the Charter's sponsor/PM fields.
- Submitting the A5 skeleton as the complete WBS or omitting PM work.
- Unmeasurable objectives or criteria ([[smart-objectives]]).
