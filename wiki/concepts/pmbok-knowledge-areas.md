# PMBOK knowledge areas

The ten knowledge areas as presented in [[gpe-slides-3-2026-2027]]:

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Integration | Unify, consolidate and articulate all the elements |
| Scope | Include all the work required, **and only** the work required |
| Schedule | Timely completion |
| Cost | Estimate, budget and control costs within the approved budget |
| Quality | Satisfy the needs the project was undertaken for (conformance to requirements and fitness for use) |
| Human Resources | Organize, manage and lead the team |
| Communications | Plan, collect, create, distribute, store, retrieve and dispose of project information |
| Risk | Plan, identify, analyse, plan responses, monitor and control risk |
| Procurement | Acquire products/services from outside the team |
| Stakeholders | Identify, analyse expectations and impact, engage stakeholders |

Areas × [[project-life-cycle-and-process-groups]] = 49 processes (PMBOK 6th).

## Famous failures by weak area (slide exercise)

Sydney Opera House (A$7M/1963 → A$102M/1973) → cost & schedule (stated on the slide). Others posed for voting: Hubble mirror 2.2 µm too flat (quality), Mars Climate Orbiter lb-f vs newtons (communication), Boeing 787 with 50+ suppliers and a 2008→2011 slip (procurement), Stuttgart 21 protests (stakeholders), BER design changes during construction (scope). *The mappings after the first are the wiki's interpretation of the slide labels.*

## Drill (PC2)

Supplier contract → procurement; report frequency to sponsor → communication; probability of supplier delay → risk; staying within budget → cost; making areas work together → integration ([[pc2-gpe-2026-2027]]).
