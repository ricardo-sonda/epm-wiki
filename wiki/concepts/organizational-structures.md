# Organizational structures

How projects link to the parent organization ([[gpe-slides-1-2026-2027]]).

| Structure | How it works | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Functional** | Project work done inside functional departments | Deep expertise, specialization; little organizational change | Slow cross-department communication; conflicting departmental priorities |
| **Projectized** | Dedicated teams report directly to PMs | Faster decisions, easier communication, cohesion, holistic optimization | Resource duplication/underuse, allocation conflicts, careers tied to project success |
| **Matrix** | People report to both PM and functional manager (often with a PMO) | Combines both; suits multiple orientations, high information needs, shared resources (Mullins 1999) | Multiple-boss problem; higher management complexity |

Projectized structures focus responsibility and authority, ease control/coordination and improve communication and customer relationships (Meredith & Mantel 2011, via Ulusoy & Hazır 2021).
