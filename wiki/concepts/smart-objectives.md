# SMART objectives

Project objectives in the [[project-charter]] should be stated in measurable terms with success criteria ([[gpe-slides-4-2026-2027]]). The course tests this with SMART classification exercises; failing criteria cited in the answers are lack of specificity, measurability, achievability and a deadline (time-bound).

## PC2 worked examples ([[pc2-gpe-2026-2027]])

| Objective | Verdict | Rewrite |
|---|---|---|
| "Improve the quality of our service." | Not specific, measurable or time-bound | Raise satisfaction from 7.2 to 8.0/10 by Dec 2027 |
| "Reduce energy consumption in Building A by 15% by December 2027, vs 2025." | SMART | — |
| "Become the best hospital in Portugal." | "Best" unmeasurable, no deadline, maybe unachievable | Top 5 in the national patient-satisfaction survey by 2028 |
| "Train all 40 nurses on the new triage software by March 2027." | SMART | — |
| "Increase website traffic significantly next year." | "Significantly" unmeasurable | +25% monthly visits by Dec 2027 vs 2026 average |

## Slide examples (posed, not answered)

Increase detergent sales by 3% next year; enter mobile telecoms as a "quality operator"; loyalty card by April with 6M members by early summer; max waiting time 20 min with 95% service level.

## SmartXCharge hooks

Measurable targets available from the case: completion by Week 32 (tolerance < 1 week); plan approved by end of Week 4; pilot thresholds (~30 chargers, ≥100 sessions, ≥3 battery scenarios); portal initial load ≤3 s; internal cost within 5% of baseline. See [[smartxcharge-planning-anchors]].
