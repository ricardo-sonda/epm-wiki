# Portfolio, program, project

([[gpe-slides-1-2026-2027]])

- **Portfolio:** projects, programs and operations grouped to achieve strategic objectives; components need not be related. **Project portfolio management (PPM)** identifies, prioritizes, authorizes, manages and controls them.
- **Program:** related projects managed in a coordinated way to obtain benefits not available individually (Apollo 11 within the Apollo program).
- Hierarchy: strategic plan → portfolio → programs → projects. Strategy flows from corporate to project level (Morris & Jamieson 2005).

## Classroom cases

- Hospital group with €20M for €35M of unrelated proposals (MRI, cloud email, car park, ISO 9001, paediatric wing) → **portfolio** (the wing may itself be a program).
- New metro line (tunnels, stations, 18 trains, signalling, driver training) → **program**: no part delivers the benefit alone.
- PC1: evaluating unrelated ideas by strategic alignment and ROI → **portfolio management** ([[pc1-gpe-2026-2027]]).

## Project selection

Selection steps: strategic compatibility, technical feasibility, cost and benefit estimates (tangible/intangible), economic-financial feasibility; go/no-go after a feasibility study.

**NPV under a budget:** with €200k and projects A–G, the slide answer is **A-C-E-G** (investment €164k, NPV €139k).

**Weighted multi-criteria scoring:** Sᵢ = Σⱼ sᵢⱼ·wⱼ with weights summing to 1; assumes additive, preference-independent criteria; pick the highest score. ISP example weights: speed 0.60, service quality 0.25, setup cost 0.12, monthly fee 0.03. *Derived calculation (not on the slide):* ISP A = 41.2, ISP B = 35.25, ISP C = 23.55 → ISP A.
