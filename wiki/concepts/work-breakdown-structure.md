# Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

The WBS breaks the project work into smaller, more manageable pieces (work packages). Breaking the work down is a **core planning responsibility of the PM**, done with team input because members know the technical work ([[pc1-gpe-2026-2027]]). *The scope lecture (T5) is not yet ingested, so formal definitions and decomposition rules are pending.*

## WBS dictionary

Per work package: *Work Package*, *Description*, *Completion criterion / Milestone (if applicable)* — a short description and a clear completion criterion, identifying formal milestones ([[pm-template-d1]]).

## SmartXCharge requirements

- Top level = the client's Annex A5 phases (Initiation & Discovery; Architecture & Integrations; MVP Build; System Testing; UAT/Lisbon Pilot; Pilot Assessment & Rollout Decision; Hardening & National Rollout; Stabilization & Closure). These must be **retained**, but they are **not** the complete WBS ([[delivery-1-instructions]]).
- Add all AES work packages needed to deliver **and manage** the project, explicitly including **project-management work packages** (planning, control/reporting, closure).
- Natural candidates from the case ([[smartxcharge-business-case-2026-2027]]): BRD & acceptance criteria; high-level architecture; integration plan; test & pilot plan; mobile app and web portal builds; charger, battery and finance/asset integrations; security/privacy assessment (before pilot); training plan and delivery; user/admin guide; cutover & handover checklists; pilot report; hypercare. *These are the wiki's suggestions drawn from Annex A, not a prescribed list.*
- In Delivery 2 the WBS is loaded into MS Project and decomposed into schedulable activities ([[delivery-2-instructions]]).
