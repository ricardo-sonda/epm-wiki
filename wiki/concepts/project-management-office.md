# Project Management Office (PMO)

An organizational body responsible for the centralized, coordinated management of the projects or programs under its domain ([[gpe-slides-1-2026-2027]]).

## Functions

Develop PM methodology, practices and standards; coach, mentor and train PMs; keep the portfolio aligned with strategy; manage shared resources; coordinate cross-project communication; support closure, reporting and performance assessment. Services include project support, consultancy, training, software tools and methods/standards.

## Types

- **Supporting:** helps PMs with methodologies, reports, policies, templates, training.
- **Controlling:** active decision role ensuring policies and best practices are followed.
- **Managing:** fully responsible for project success; considerable control over PMs.

## Classroom cases

- João is reassigned by the PMO head, whose appraisal sets his bonus, and the PMO reports all projects to the board → *managing* PMO (by the definitions above).
- Ana's kick-off is blocked until her risk register is complete and her folder is audited against templates → *controlling* PMO (by the definitions above).

The slide poses these as questions; the classifications are the wiki's reading of the definitions.
