# Project steering committee

An organizational entity providing executive oversight, strategic direction, advice and guidance for one or more projects, made up of key stakeholders (e.g. representatives of participating organizations) who usually do not work on the project themselves ([[gpe-slides-1-2026-2027]]).

## Roles

- Provide input to and approve the project charter.
- Oversee execution.
- Make key decisions (e.g. scope changes).
- Mandate and give ongoing direction to the PM.

## Cautionary case: Berlin-Brandenburg Airport (BER)

Opening planned 2011, actual 2020; budget €2.4bn → ~€6bn; opening cancelled five times. Board of politicians with little construction expertise; weak self-regulated controlling (learned late about fire-protection failures); general contractor dropped and works split into lots, many design changes during construction; political pressure on dates. Each failure maps to one of the roles above.

## SmartXCharge

Monthly, 60-minute committee chaired by the LightEnergy COO (Customer Sponsor), with the Client PM, Client IT representative and Supplier PM; the AES Executive Sponsor joins for major escalations. It makes the gate decisions, including the Go/No-Go for national rollout after the [[lisbon-pilot]], recorded in signed minutes ([[smartxcharge-business-case-2026-2027]]). Relevant to the Delivery 3 meetings plan.
