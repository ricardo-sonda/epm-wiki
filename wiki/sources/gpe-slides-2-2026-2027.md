# EPM Slides 2 — The Project Manager

Chapter 2: who the project manager should be and what they do, organised around three roles — **lead a team**, **manage expectations and relationships**, **manage risk** — plus professional certification.

## Key content

- Desirable PM skills (Lockyer & Gordon 2005), technical and human; PM tasks (plan, recruit/coordinate, analyse interfaces and constraints, budget, run, control, deliver). → [[project-manager-competences]]
- Manager vs leader; leadership (Verma & Wilderman 1994) as IQ + emotional intelligence (Stein 2009).
- Stakeholder management: commitment, engagement, buy-in; internal vs external stakeholder lists; a different "buy-in" plan per stakeholder group. → [[stakeholder-analysis]]
- Team development (Tuckman 1965) and expected team-member traits.
- Uncertainty types (De Meyer et al. 2002: variation, foreseen, unforeseen, chaos); risk definition (PMI 2017) and risk vs uncertainty (March & Simon 1958). → [[risk-and-uncertainty]]
- Certification: PMI (CAPM, PMP, PgMP; PMP needs a bachelor degree, 3 years' experience, 35 h training; 4 h / 200-question exam) and IPMA levels D–A (ICB: technical, contextual, behavioural competences).
