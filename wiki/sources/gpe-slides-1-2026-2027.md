# EPM Slides 1 — Concepts, Success Factors, Environment

Chapter 1 of the course in three parts: 1.1 Concepts and Definitions, 1.2 Critical Success Factors, 1.3 The Project's Environment (with a conclusion on stakeholders, steering committees and PMOs). The largest lecture deck ingested so far (~60 slides).

## 1.1 Concepts and definitions

- Definitions of a project from PMI (PMBOK 7th), IPMA (ICB 3.0) and ISO 21500; project vs operations ("transform the business" vs "run the business"). → [[what-is-a-project]]
- Triple constraint ("fatal triangle": cost, scope/quality, time) and the five dimensions (scope, quality, time, cost, risk), with Christmas-party and office-computerisation examples. → [[project-dimensions-and-triple-constraint]]
- Project-oriented vs operations-oriented organizations; functional, matrix and projectized structures. → [[organizational-structures]]
- Project management as a discipline: one goal, one project, one leader, one team, one organization; the PM leads a team, manages expectations/relationships, manages risk. → [[project-manager-competences]]

## 1.2 Critical success factors

- Success = meet expectations; on time, on budget, to spec; differs per stakeholder.
- Shenhar et al. (2001) four success dimensions; Standish CHAOS data (~3 in 10 IT projects succeed); PMI 2024 value-based success (47% for IT); success by industry/type/funding.
- Failure statistics and causes (lack of user input, incomplete and changing requirements…); communication and requirements as the core problems; scope creep. → [[project-success-and-failure]], [[change-control-and-scope-creep]]

## 1.3 The project's environment

- Corporate strategy → project strategy (Morris & Jamieson 2005).
- Portfolio vs program vs project; hospital (portfolio) vs metro line (program) exercise; project selection by NPV under a budget and by weighted multi-criteria scoring. → [[portfolio-program-project]]
- Stakeholders, customer vs sponsor (JFK/Apollo, Bootmaker exercise). → [[stakeholder-analysis]]
- Steering committee roles and the Berlin BER failure. → [[steering-committee]]
- PMO functions and types (supporting, controlling, managing). → [[project-management-office]]
