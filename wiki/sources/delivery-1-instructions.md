# Delivery 1 Instructions

Instructions for the first group-project delivery: a first version of the SmartXCharge Project Plan covering the **Project Charter, Stakeholder Analysis and Project Scope (WBS + WBS Dictionary)**, written in the [[pm-template-d1]] template. The operational checklist is on [[delivery-1-charter-stakeholders-scope]].

## Key points

- Read the [[smartxcharge-business-case-2026-2027]] and Annex A (Client RFP Summary) and extract all relevant project information.
- **Positioning:** the group acts as AES's *Supplier* Project Manager responding to LightEnergy's RFP. In the Charter, *Sponsor* and *Project Manager* refer to AES roles; client governance roles go in the Stakeholder Analysis.
- Fixed-price contract of €650,000 = AES revenue, paid by milestones. AES must internalize delivery costs and secure a sustainable margin; keep schedule/scope/quality consistent with internal costs and billing milestones across all deliveries.
- WBS: keep the client's Annex A5 phases as the top level and add every AES work package needed to deliver **and manage** the project (including project-management work packages). Complete a WBS Dictionary for all work packages.
- The Charter's schedule and **Initial Internal Cost Estimate** are high-level; the estimate is AES's internal delivery cost, **not** the €650,000, and is refined in Delivery 2.
- Where the case is silent, make reasonable assumptions, state them explicitly, and keep them consistent in later deliveries.
