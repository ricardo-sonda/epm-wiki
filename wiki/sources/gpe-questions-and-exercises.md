# EPM Questions and Exercises (handout)

The course exercise handout (12 pages), used in the practical classes. Contains **problem statements only, no solutions**; worked answers for sections 1–2 appear in [[pc1-gpe-2026-2027]].

## Sections

1. **Project management scope** (1.1–1.3): Autoeuropa, beverage company, computer reseller. → [[what-is-a-project]]
2. **Success and failure** (2.1–2.4): consulting PM, new product, XPTO software house, architect's villa. → [[project-success-and-failure]]
3. **Project goal and business justification** (3.1–3.4): Good-Clothes loyalty card (3 months), Enderola sales force for a new line, In-Front partner intranet, Lump of Sugar convenience-store pilot (2 stores by March). → [[project-charter]]
4. **Scope statement** (4.1–4.3): TotalService service-quality study (€50k budget, €4k study), Health-at-Home December campaign (€50k), Mastercook CRM (€60k, +45% sales in 3 years). → [[project-charter]]
5. **Work Breakdown Structure:** chocolate-chain product launch in Portugal with 17 activities (A–Q: campaign, agency, training programme and materials, print shop, press conference, ad campaigns, stock, launch ceremony, sales monitoring, evaluation report). → [[work-breakdown-structure]]
6. **Gantt charts / critical path** (6.1–6.9): precedence tables for duration, critical path and floats; deadline back-scheduling (6.6, 6.7); PERT three-point estimates with probability of finishing in 32 days and the 90% date (6.8); PROACTIV "New Direction" weekly plan with a resource-availability constraint (6.9).
7. **Resource levelling** (7.1–7.4): consultant/programmer histograms under availability limits, overtime (+2 h or +4 h/day) and subcontracting costs (€30/h normal, €45/h overtime, €50/h subcontract in 7.4); rule that duration and critical activities may not change.
8. **Crashing** (8.1–8.2): least-cost crashing to 40 weeks; MX project with €5,000/week penalty beyond six weeks.
9. **Earned Value Management** (9.1–9.3): EAC for a task, a store renovation (15 days, €630k), and the SUCCESS consultancy market study (€26k incl. €2.1k reserve).

## Gaps
