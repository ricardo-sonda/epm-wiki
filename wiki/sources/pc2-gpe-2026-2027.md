# PC2 — Practical Class Week 2

Exercise class (11 Nov 2026) on PM competences and the PM life cycle: ten short exercises with answers.

## Exercises and answers

1. Uncertainty types: late redesign request → unforeseen; flood → chaos; occasional late deliveries → variation; announced strike → foreseen. → [[risk-and-uncertainty]]
2. Internal: sponsor, functional manager, team member, top management. External: suppliers, regulatory body, end users, shareholders. → [[stakeholder-analysis]]
3. Emotional intelligence in meetings: check in privately with a quiet/frustrated member, stay calm with bad news, adapt communication, praise in public and criticise in private, manage your own frustration. → [[project-manager-competences]]
4. Certifications give shared vocabulary, baseline proof, credibility, structure and networks, but test knowledge more than performance — "necessary but not sufficient".
5. Process groups ↔ purposes (Initiating → define/authorize; Planning → scope and course of action; Executing → coordinate resources; M&C → track and start changes; Closing → finish and obtain acceptance). → [[project-life-cycle-and-process-groups]]
6. Situations ↔ knowledge areas: supplier contract → procurement; report frequency → communication; supplier-delay probability → risk; budget → cost; making areas work together → integration. → [[pmbok-knowledge-areas]]
7. True/false: the charter is **not** a detailed plan; assumptions are **not** verified facts (each is a risk source); resistant stakeholders should be engaged, not excluded. → [[project-charter]]
8. SMART classification with rewrites. → [[smart-objectives]]
9. Business needs: mobile payments → technological advance; EU emission rules → legal; rural schools → social; merging departments → organizational; utility power line → customer request.
10. Lisbon bike-sharing power/interest matrix: city council = key player; residents/shopkeepers = keep informed; transport regulator = keep satisfied; future users = keep informed and involve in pilots.
