# EPM Slides 0 — Course Presentation

Opening lecture of Engineering Project Management (EPM/GPE), MEGE/MEGIE/Min-EG/Min-GIS, 2nd quarter 2026/2027, IST (Department of Engineering and Management). Covers faculty, learning goals, syllabus, evaluation, key dates and bibliography. Course logistics are consolidated on [[epm-course]].

## Key content

- **Faculty:** Prof. Rafael Miranda (theory, Mon & Wed; office hours Wed 11:00–12:00, Pavilhão Central 1st floor, DEG); Prof. Edgar Mascarenhas (practical exercise classes, Wed); Prof. Pedro Leão (group-assignment support, Tue).
- **Career framing:** cites PMI's Global PM Talent Gap (2025) and Salary Survey 14th ed. (2025): PMP holders earn a premium over non-PMP (e.g. US +23.7%, Germany +18.9%); Portugal median ≈ US$41k (Payscale). See [[project-manager-competences]].
- **Learning goals:** recognize when PM is the right approach; identify critical success factors; initiate a project with stakeholders and expectations; plan for scope/time/budget; manage risk; control with Earned Value Management (EVM); close a project; use MS Project.
- **Syllabus (T = lecture):** projects and relevance (T1–T3); the PM and skills (T3); life cycle (T4); initiation (T4); planning — scope (T5), time & resources (T6–T7), cost (T8–T9), risk (T10); monitoring & control and closure (T11); Agile (T12).
- **Evaluation:** group project 40% (min 9.5/20, groups of 6–7, peer assessment on a 7-level scale None→Extreme) + two 60-min tests 60% (min average 8/20; exam replaces both).
- **Dates (as stated on the slides):** groups enrolled 11 Nov 2026; D1 20 Nov; D2 4 Dec; D3 oral + report 16 Dec 2026; T60-1 26 Nov (up to T6); T60-2 17 Dec (T6–T12); exam 25 Jan 2027. Some delivery dates differ from the delivery instruction documents — see [[epm-course]].
- **Bibliography:** PMBOK 6th ed. (2017); Ulusoy & Hazır (2021) *An Introduction to Project Modeling and Planning*; Verzuh (2012) *The Fast Forward MBA in Project Management*; Kerzner (2006) *Project Management: A Systems Approach*.
