# EPM Slides 4 — Initiating

Chapter 4: the Initiating process group and its two key outputs, the **Project Charter** and the **Stakeholder Register**. Directly relevant to [[delivery-1-charter-stakeholders-scope]].

## Key content

- Initiating = define a new project and obtain authorization; align stakeholder expectations with the project's purpose.
- Main processes: select PM, review prior analyses, set the team, assimilate the business case, identify stakeholders, develop and approve the charter.
- Charter = consolidation of what is known, a "contract" between team and stakeholders; with an external customer it is usually **not** the commercial contract (negotiated after an RFP response). → [[project-charter]]
- Business needs that trigger projects (market demand, organizational need, customer request, technological advance, legal requirement, ecological impact, social need).
- SMART objectives exercise. → [[smart-objectives]]
- Customer priorities, assumptions and constraints (essential to risk planning).
- Nine-element charter structure (Ulusoy & Hazır 2021) and the Crossrail delivery-strategy critique (weakest elements: unmeasurable objectives and no early risk analysis).
- Stakeholder register (who, expectations, impacts, power, communication), the seven-column stakeholder analysis table, and the power/interest matrix. → [[stakeholder-analysis]]
