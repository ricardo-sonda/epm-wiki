# EPM Slides 3 — The Project Management Life Cycle

Chapter 3: the generic life cycle, the five PMBOK process groups, the ten knowledge areas, and their 49-process mapping (PMBOK 6th ed.).

## Key content

- Life cycle = series of phases from initiation to closure; MSc-thesis analogy (topic → plan → research/writing → defence). → [[project-life-cycle-and-process-groups]]
- What happens in planning, executing and controlling; how effort, people, risk/uncertainty and **cost of changes** evolve over time — changes are cheapest before execution (BER airport counter-example). → [[change-control-and-scope-creep]]
- PM life cycle vs execution (product) life cycle (conception → design → development → tests → implementation).
- Five process groups (PMBOK 5th): Initiating, Planning, Executing, Monitoring & Controlling, Closing.
- Ten knowledge areas with definitions; famous-failure exercise mapping cases to areas (Sydney Opera House → cost & schedule; Hubble; Mars Climate Orbiter; Boeing 787; Stuttgart 21; BER). → [[pmbok-knowledge-areas]]
- IST student-residence exercise mapping activities to process groups.
