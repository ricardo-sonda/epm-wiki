# PM Template D1

The Word template groups must fill in for [[delivery-1-charter-stakeholders-scope]]. Its sections define exactly what Delivery 1 must contain.

## Structure

1. **Identification:** group number; student numbers and names.
2. **Project Charter table:** Name; Business area; Customer; Initial Internal Cost Estimate; Supplier Project Manager; Start date; Supplier Executive Sponsor; Finished date.
3. **Project Goal:** a brief, outcome-oriented description.
4. **Expected business benefits:** table of *Benefit* / *How it is obtained*.
5. **Project Management Success Criteria:** *Criteria* / *Metrics* (how success is measured).
6. **Project Management Success Factors:** *Success Factor* / *Responsible* (who can guarantee the condition).
7. **Goals and products:** intermediate goals; intermediate and final products and deliverables.
8. **Product and project requirements and specifications:** characteristics, features, success metrics, conditions.
9. **Assumptions and constraints:** group assumptions plus internal/external constraints.
10. **Initial Internal Cost Estimate:** Manpower; Materials; Suppliers and service providers; Financial costs; Administration; Other costs; TOTAL.
11. **Stakeholder Analysis table** — columns: Stakeholder; interest in the project; potential impact on the project; what the project expects them to provide; perceived attitudes and/or risks; inputs to the communication plan; who manages the relationship. Same columns as the lecture's register (see [[stakeholder-analysis]]).
12. **WBS:** Annex A5 phases as top level, completed with AES work packages.
13. **WBS Dictionary:** per work package — *Work Package*, *Description*, *Completion criterion / Milestone (if applicable)*.

## Notes

- Success *criteria* (measurable) are distinct from success *factors* (enabling conditions with an owner); see [[project-success-and-failure]].
- Assumptions are unverified beliefs and therefore risk sources (see [[project-charter]]).
