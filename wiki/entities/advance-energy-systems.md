# Advance Energy Systems (AES)

The **supplier/provider** in the [[smartxcharge-project]] case, awarded the SmartXCharge contract by [[lightenergy]]. AES designs, integrates and delivers the solution. Student groups act as AES's Supplier Project Manager ([[delivery-1-instructions]]).

## Responsibilities

- Turn LightEnergy's expectations into a credible delivery plan: detailed WBS, schedule, resource calendars, critical path and internal cost baseline (the client only provides a phase skeleton).
- Prepare and execute the work that supports each client decision gate.
- Maintain a weekly one-pager between gates (progress, plan vs actual, cost vs internal baseline, risks/blockers, decisions needed).
- Assess change requests before any commitment (see [[change-control-and-scope-creep]]).

## Internal economics

- Revenue: €650,000 fixed price, invoiced at milestones.
- Costs it bears: AES direct labor (daily rates listed in [[smartxcharge-planning-anchors]]), other justified direct costs, and 12% corporate & management overhead on direct labor ([[delivery-2-instructions]]).
- Target: final internal cost within 5% of the approved internal baseline; the difference between revenue and internal cost is the planned margin. See [[fixed-price-contract-and-internal-cost-baseline]].

## People and location

Supplier Executive Sponsor (Head/Director of Delivery/Professional Services) and Supplier PM; delivery team of Solution Architect, Integration Lead, Test Lead, Systems Security Specialist, two Developers, Data & Reporting Analyst and Trainer. Works mostly off-site; meetings and key-user training in Lisbon (optionally Porto).
