# Lisbon Pilot

The four-week user-acceptance pilot of [[smartxcharge-project]] at LightEnergy's Lisbon hub — "the real test" for the client, and the event tied to **50% of the contract payment** ([[smartxcharge-business-case-2026-2027]]).

## Timing

- Target start **around Week 20** (Week 0 = 4 Jan 2027); duration 4 weeks.
- Preceded by the *Lisbon Pilot Authorized* gate; followed by *Lisbon Pilot Accepted* and the Steering Committee's **Go/No-Go for National Rollout**, based on objective evidence in signed minutes.
- A **privacy assessment must be completed before the pilot starts** (Annex A2) — a predecessor worth modelling explicitly in the schedule.

## Minimum conditions (Annex A4)

- End-to-end operation with real assets, automatic month-end postings to finance, dashboards showing savings against an agreed baseline, operator-visible status/alerts.
- ~30 chargers active; ≥100 sessions completed; ≥3 battery scenarios (e.g. reduce peak, shift load); one failover/rollback drill and one recovery drill.

## Expected evidence

Stable day-to-day operations, measurable savings vs baseline, prompt admin responses, data protection (SSO, RBAC, encryption, audit trail), restorable backups, confident key users after short hands-on training, and an IT team able to monitor integrations. Output: Pilot Report (Executive Summary) with KPIs, lessons learned and next steps.

## Planning notes

Required milestones in Delivery 2 include *Lisbon Pilot Authorized* and *Lisbon Pilot Accepted*; billing milestone 50% = €325,000 at *Lisbon Pilot Accepted* ([[delivery-2-instructions]]). The pilot is a natural focus for the Delivery 3 top-5 risks (e.g. client environment/data access, privacy assessment slip, pilot activity thresholds). See [[smartxcharge-planning-anchors]].
