# LightEnergy

The **client/customer** in the [[smartxcharge-project]] case: a leading (fictional) Portuguese energy operator with four operational hubs (North, Center, Lisbon, Algarve), pursuing selective expansion into European markets with rising EV adoption and dynamic tariffs ([[smartxcharge-business-case-2026-2027]]).

## Situation

- Several hundred EVs; roughly 200 charge points of different makes.
- On-site battery in Lisbon used for peak shaving with basic, rule-of-thumb orchestration.
- Data fragmented across finance/asset system, identity tools and ad-hoc spreadsheets.
- Peak-hour exposure and occasional contracted-power overruns create energy-bill volatility.
- Chose to buy rather than build: awarded the contract to [[advance-energy-systems]] after procurement.

## Role in the project

- Retains ownership of the business need, controls access to systems/environments, and makes the progression decisions (requirements/architecture review, pilot authorization, rollout Go/No-Go).
- Provides environments and representative data under its security and data-protection policies.
- Pays €650,000 in milestone instalments (see [[smartxcharge-planning-anchors]]).
- Client roles (COO sponsor, Client PM, Product Owner Energy & Fleet, IT/Security representative) cost AES €0 in the internal cost plan ([[delivery-2-instructions]]).
- The RFP (Annex A) is the reference against which delivery, acceptance and the rollout decision are judged.
