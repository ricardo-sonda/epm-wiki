## Syllabus

T1–T3 projects and relevance; T3 the PM; T4 life cycle and initiation; T5 scope; T6–T7 time and resources; T8–T9 cost; T10 risk; T11 monitoring, control and closure; T12 Agile. Ingested so far: chapters 1–4 (T1–T4). Bibliography: PMBOK 6th ed.; Ulusoy & Hazır (2021); Verzuh (2012); Kerzner (2006).
